ignore-above-mapping-change: Elasticsearch 9.5.5 lets PUT _mapping change a keyword's `ignore_above`, and the new limit applies only to documents indexed afterwards (earlier documents keep the terms, doc values and `_ignored` entries they were indexed with). es-mini re-applies the current limit to every document in memory (es-index/src/postings.rs builds Indexed::Exact postings from the current mapping), while on-disk segments keep the limit they were written with, so after a change memory, disk and ES disagree, and a reopen replaying the translog under the new mapping disagrees again. Record ES's answers (term, exists, range, sort, terms aggs, `_ignored`) for documents indexed before and after raising and lowering the limit (skill: verify-with-elasticsearch), then make es-mini give them in memory, after a flush, and after reopen (record the limit each document was indexed with, or whatever ES's model needs).
